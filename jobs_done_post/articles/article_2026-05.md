# Jobs Done - May 2026 Product Performance

Hi Team,

Here is how our key product usage KPI, Jobs Done, performed in May across two platforms.

---

## High level overview

✅ Jobs Done

- LumApps 111.7M (-6.1% MoM, +32.0% YoY)
- Beekeeper 35.1M (+3.2% MoM, -0.3% YoY)

👨‍🦲 MAU

- LumApps 2.79M (-1.0% MoM)
- Beekeeper 693.5K (flat MoM)

---

## LumApps

Note that for LumApps, Jobs Done currently covers only a handful of features and only within the Communication & Collaboration product domain, so a decline in May does not indicate a platform-wide pullback but just a change in specific product domain engagement.

📄 **Content** continues to be the engine — 105M Jobs Done and +33.3% YoY. May's -3.5% follows the same seasonal pattern as last year, when summer brought a natural dip before a strong autumn recovery.

🏠 **Spaces** and 📝 **Posts** both pulled back in May (-13.4% and -12.8% MoM respectively), continuing a soft patch that mirrors the same period in 2025. Both are still growing year-over-year (+6.2% and +6.6%), so this looks seasonal rather than structural.

## Beekeeper

Beekeeper's volume was broadly stable in May at 35.1M, with most of the portfolio holding steady. Two features stood out:

💬 **Chats** keeps growing — 6.9M Jobs Done, +7.7% MoM and +8.8% YoY. What makes it more interesting is that frequency also rose (28.9x → 30.5x), meaning users aren't just showing up more — they're engaging more deeply per session.

☑️ **Tasks** (Work & Automation) is on a five-month growth streak and has more than doubled in the past year (+105.7% YoY). It's the clearest signal in the portfolio that Beekeeper's automation capabilities are finding a real, growing audience.

📡 On the flip side, **Streams** — Beekeeper's highest-volume feature — is holding steady month-to-month but is -9.3% versus May 2025. With Chats growing and Streams softening on an annual basis, it's worth keeping an eye on how communication patterns evolve across the two features over the coming months.
