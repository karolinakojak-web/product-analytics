## LumApps

Note that for LumApps, Jobs Done currently covers only a handful of features and only within the Communication & Collaboration product domain.

📄 **Content** is rising again and has even reached the best KPI of the current year with 118.9M JD (+13.2% MoM, +37.5% YoY) with both users (+2.2%) and frequency (+10.8%) growing together.

🏠 **Spaces** and 📝 **Posts** are both on the rise, making June the third-best month of the year so far with 3.44M JD (+18.5% MoM) for Spaces and 1.71M JD (+22.1% MoM) for Posts.

🗨️ **Comments** and 👍 **Reactions**: after a really hard pull back in May they finally increase a lot too. 348.8K JD (+141.6% MoM) for Comments and 4.9M JD (+172.5% MoM) for Reactions.

*Full breakdown available in the [LumApps Jobs Done dashboard](https://bi.lumapps.com/dashboards/base::jobs_done?tab_name=Jobs+Done&Time+Frame=13+month+ago+for+13+month&Product+Domain+Group=Communication+%26+Collaboration).*

## Beekeeper

☑️ **Tasks** (Work & Automation) is still on a six-month growth streak with +6.7% MoM and +125.5% YoY, and we still have more active users (+6.1% MoM).

🗂️ **Forms** (Work & Automation) keeps growing, +10% MoM and +43.5% YoY, with a frequency in constant increase for six months.

🔗 Contrariwise, **Shortcuts** (Work & Automation) is on a six-month decrease after a six-month growth at the end of 2025. But it is still growing year-over-year: -7.2% MoM | +6.6% YoY.
