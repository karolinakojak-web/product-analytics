## LumApps

Note that for LumApps, Jobs Done currently covers only a handful of features and only within the Communication & Collaboration product domain.

📄 **Content** slightly declined in July compared to May, but it's the second-best month of the year so far, so it's really good for this first summer holiday month: 119.5M JD (-0.5% MoM, +41.6% YoY) but higher frequency (+1.6%).

🎬 **Videos**: after 2 months of pull back in April and May, Videos slightly increased in June (+3% MoM) and July (+1.4% MoM), but it's still quite stable since September 2025.

*Full breakdown available in the [LumApps Jobs Done dashboard](https://bi.lumapps.com/dashboards/base::jobs_done?tab_name=Jobs+Done&Time+Frame=13+month+ago+for+13+month&Product+Domain+Group=Communication+%26+Collaboration).*

## Beekeeper

📊 **Surveys** (People & Growth) -20.9% MoM with both users (-16.7%) and frequency (-5.1%) declining together. The last 3 months were quite stable, but we have fewer active users for this first summer holidays month.
